Process

From product photo to finished ad, without losing the product.

A simple, repeatable workflow that keeps every asset on-brand and ready to test.

1
Lock the product
Start from real packshots. Label, logo, cap shape and colour are the fixed reference for every generation.
2
Concept & hook
A clear idea and a first-three-seconds hook, written before any prompt is run.
3
Generate
Image-to-video with prompts shaped for lighting, texture and camera movement, then selects.
4
Edit & finish
Cuts, captions, sound and colour in CapCut. Product text is checked frame by frame.
5
Version for testing
Alternate hooks, lengths and formats so the team can learn what performs.
Creative approach

Three concepts I build for almost any brand.

Reusable starting points that I adapt to each brand’s product, tone and audience.

Concept 1
Close-up first
Macro shots of the product catching light, built from the real packshot so the packaging stays exact.
Hook idea: “Watch this before you buy another one.”
Concept 2
Everyday ritual
A calm lifestyle moment that shows the product in real use, with warm light and natural movement.
Hook idea: “The small habit I never skip.”
Concept 3
Explain it simply
A short visual explainer on what makes the product different, kept clear, simple and within advertising rules.
Hook idea: “What is it, actually?”

About

Hi, I’m Jana.

I’m an AI content creator based in Egypt, using image and video generation tools to make product, beauty and lifestyle visuals that look polished and real. My favourite moment is when a still product photo starts to move and feel alive, and I take care that the packaging, label and colours stay exactly right.

I work independently and remotely, enjoy contributing ideas as much as following a brief, and keep to agreed deadlines. I communicate comfortably in English and I’m always keen to try new tools and formats to find what works best for a brand.
